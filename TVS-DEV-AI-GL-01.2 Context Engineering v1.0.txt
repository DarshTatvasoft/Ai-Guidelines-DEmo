TatvaSoft   Context Engineering   TVS - DEV - AI - GL - 01.2 | v1.0 | 25.09.2026  TVS · Internal   Page   4   of   6  Introduction  K E Y T AK E AW A Y  Context engineering is simply the habit of giving the AI   only the exact information it needs   for the job right  now — nothing more, nothing less.  Working with AI coding tools isn't just about crafting the perfect prompt — it’s mostly about   managing what the  AI can see   at any given moment.  H OW T HE A I RE A D S C ON TEXT  AI models don't "think" like humans do; they read the  entire chat history and every attached file   on   every  single turn .  W HEN CO NTEX T GET S C LU T TERE D  When that space gets cluttered with   old code, failed  attempts, or off - topic chatter , the AI gets confused  and starts making mistakes.  Here are a few simple Do’s and Don’ts to help you get better, faster results from your AI editor.

TatvaSoft   Context Engineering   TVS - DEV - AI - GL - 01.2 | v1.0 | 25.09.2026  TVS · Internal   Page   5   of   6  Do’s  Start a fresh chat when switching tasks or when debugging gets noisy  •   Treat AI conversations as   temporary space for a single task .  •   If you’ve finished a feature, or if three bug fixes in a row have failed,   clear the slate .  •   Carry forward only the   core problem   and the   latest error message   into a new thread.  Point to files instead of repasting them  •   If your AI editor already has indexing or file access, refer to files   by path   or   @mention   them directly.  •   Save your prompt space for explaining   what   you want changed and   why .  Lock in project rules using persistent configurations  •   Use   .cursorrules , system prompts, or workspace settings for guidelines that   never change — like code style,  stack preferences, or linting requirements.  ➜   This keeps your daily prompts short and focused.  Document decisions in your repo, not in chat history  •   When an experiment works out or a key architecture decision is made,   write it down in your README or  docs .  ➜   Chat logs disappear; repository documentation lasts.  Define strict boundaries before unleashing AI Agents  •   Before letting an AI agent run,   set clear boundaries .  Tell it:  What it's allowed to edit   What it shouldn't touch   What a successful fix actually looks  like

TatvaSoft   Context Engineering   TVS - DEV - AI - GL - 01.2 | v1.0 | 25.09.2026  TVS · Internal   Page   6   of   6  Don’ts  Don’t let dead - end experiments pollute your active conversation  •   Once an approach fails or gets rejected,   don't keep dragging old, failed ideas through a long chat .  Summarize what you learned   ➜   Drop the failed code   ➜   Move forward cleanly  Don’t dump the entire codebase into the prompt  •   Throwing too much code at the AI just   confuses it , making it more likely to give   wrong answers   or   break  untouched files .  Don’t force - feed specific tools or skills if the AI can figure it out  •   Avoid over - specifying every micro - step or forcing custom skills   unless a task genuinely demands a non -  standard workflow .  •   Trust the editor   to select the right capability when the goal is clear.  Don’t rely on AI chat as your project’s single source of truth  •   Never depend on previous conversation turns to remember   business logic or complex requirements .  •   If a rule matters long - term,   commit it to your codebase or docs .  —   End of Document   —
